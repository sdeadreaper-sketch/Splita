WHAT WE BUILT

One simple tool for shared expenses.

SPLITA helps roommates quickly understand how much everyone should contribute and whether their housing costs are financially manageable.

01

Fair Cost Splitting

Calculate a straightforward per-person share from rent and utilities so everyone knows their expected contribution.

02

Affordability Check

Compare total housing expenses against a chosen percentage of income and identify when rent may be becoming too expensive.

03

Roommate Friendly

Built around shared living, SPLITA keeps the calculation simple and easy for roommates to understand.
